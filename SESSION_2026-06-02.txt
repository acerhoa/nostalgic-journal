OUTSTANDING ISSUES
------------------

1. PAGE FLIP ANIMATION (CRITICAL)
   The back face of the flipping page shows content mirrored/incorrectly positioned.

   What should happen:
   - Click right arrow -> right page flips left
   - Back of page should show the INCOMING page content (next left page)
   - Content should be correctly oriented (date on left, text readable)
   - When page lands, it should seamlessly match the live page

   What's happening:
   - Back face shows date/text mirrored and positioned wrong
   - After page lands, content "jumps" to correct position

   Attempts made:
   - scaleX(-1) on back face content wrapper - didn't work
   - scaleX(-1) on individual children - didn't work
   - Combined rotateY(180deg) scaleX(-1) on .face.back - didn't work

   Root cause: The CSS 3D transform math for the back face isn't correctly
   accounting for both the rotation AND the content mirroring needed.

   Possible solutions to try:
   - Research CSS 3D card flip examples that show content on both sides
   - Try transform-style: flat on the back face
   - Try applying the scaleX at the leaf level instead of face level
   - Consider if the transform-origin is affecting the mirror
